What it catches

Fresh ears for the structure.

Not another generic AI chat. TrackLens reads the arrangement you actually built and turns structural evidence into focused listening decisions.

01 — REPETITION

Are your drops too similar?

Compare Drop 1 vs Drop 2, Main 1 vs Main 2 and see which tracks actually changed.

02 — STRUCTURE

See the whole arc.

Intro, builds, drops, main sections and breakdowns are mapped into one readable timeline.

03 — ACTION

Listen where it matters.

Jump to a section, loop eight bars and test a reversible A/B idea without TrackLens rewriting your music.

Workflow

From “something feels off” to an exact bar.

1. Open your Live SetKeep working in the project you already have.

Safe by design

Your music stays yours.

TrackLens is built around a strict read-only philosophy. Analysis happens locally and musical content is not uploaded to a cloud service.

Local · Read-only · Deterministic

✓No clips, notes, devices, automation or mixer values are changed.
✓Navigation may move the view, track selection, playhead, loop and transport.
✓No quality score pretending to know whether your track is “good”.
